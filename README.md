# Shaper

**Shaper is a free prompt that helps you turn a fuzzy idea into a foothold — a bit of firm ground you can keep and build on.**

You tell it what you want to achieve. It asks you questions — one at a time — until four things are clear:

- **Goal** — what you want, and why it matters now
- **Success** — how you'll know it worked
- **Boundaries** — what's off limits
- **The fog** — what's already clear, where you already have solid ground, and what's still unclear

Then it hands you a short **foothold** you can save — the four parts above, written down. That's it. Shaper doesn't make plans or to-do lists; it just gives you firm ground to stand on before you dive in. It's the companion to episode 1 of the video series.

## How to use it

It's one prompt. Nothing to install, no account beyond the chat tool you already use. Works the same in **ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and Gemini**.

1. **Open [`prompt.md`](prompt.md)** and copy everything in it.
   *(On this page, click `prompt.md`, then use the copy button at the top right of the file.)*
2. **Open a new, empty chat** in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or Gemini.
3. **Paste the prompt** and send it.
4. Shaper says hello and asks its first question. **Answer honestly, one question at a time.** If you don't know something, say so — "I don't know yet" is a real answer, and it has a place to go.
5. After a handful of questions, Shaper gives you your finished **foothold**.
6. **Copy that foothold and keep it** — in a note, a document, wherever you'll find it again.

A tip: give it a real goal, not a test one. The more honest you are, the more useful the result.

### Starting from your goal right away

If you already know roughly what you want, you can paste the prompt and your goal together, for example:

> *(the pasted prompt)*
>
> I want to start selling my woodwork online next year.

Shaper will pick up from there instead of asking what you want to work on.

## Your foothold — and why to keep it

Your foothold looks like this:

```
────────────────────────────
SHAPER FOOTHOLD · selling my woodwork online
────────────────────────────

GOAL
...

SUCCESS
...

BOUNDARIES
...

THE FOG
Clear: ...
First footholds: ...
Still foggy: ...
────────────────────────────
Keep this foothold. Later steps build on it.
```

**Keep it.** This foothold is your foundation. In later episodes we build on exactly this — so the clearer it is now, the more you'll get out of what comes next. You can always paste it back into a fresh chat to pick up where you left off.

*(Inside The fog, "First footholds" are the specific spots where you already have solid ground — the toeholds within your foothold.)*

## Good to know

- **No installation, no sign-up, no cost.** It's just text you paste.
- **Your answers stay in your own chat** — with whichever tool you use. Shaper is only the prompt; it sends nothing anywhere.
- **Use it as often as you like**, for anything — a business idea, a personal project, a decision you're chewing on.
- **Adapt it freely.** It's yours to copy and change.
